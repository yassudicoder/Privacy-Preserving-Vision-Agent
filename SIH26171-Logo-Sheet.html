For the best result, use the PNG files rather than copying from this page. Each image below is ppt-assets/labelled/<name>.png — a single transparent 640×760 file with the logo above its caption, so dragging one into PowerPoint gives you both, already aligned, at full resolution. Copying a region out of a PDF gives a screen-resolution bitmap instead, which shows on a projector. Plain unlabelled marks are in ppt-assets/logos/ as PNG and SVG.

The captions are dark text on transparency, sized for a light slide. On a dark background use the unlabelled marks and set your own caption colour.
