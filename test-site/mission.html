Live spacecraft telemetry simulation

LIVE SIMULATION

This demo generates spacecraft telemetry directly in your browser. The Vision Agent analyses the data locally, protects sensitive information, and sends only safe, derived information to the AI. Nothing is fetched from the internet, and raw telemetry is never sent to the AI.

Demo data only. All names, contacts and network addresses are synthetic test values. The email and IP ranges are reserved by internet standards and can never belong to a real person.

Why this demo matters

Real spacecraft and government systems carry sensitive operational information alongside the measurements engineers actually need to reason about. Our approach lets the browser agent analyse tabular and visual information on the user's own machine, remove the sensitive parts, and send only sanitised or derived results to the AI — so the question gets answered without the data leaving.
